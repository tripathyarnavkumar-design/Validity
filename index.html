    Construct validity: convergent

    Do the items written for each construct hang together and measure one shared thing?

    AVE = Σλ² ÷ k CR = (Σλ)² ÷ [(Σλ)² + Σ(1 − λ²)] α = k/(k−1) · (1 − Σσᵢ² ÷ σₓ²)

    Criteria

    • KMO ≥ .60 (≥ .80 meritorious); Bartlett’s test p < .05
    • Loading λ ≥ .70; .40–.70 review; < .40 drop
    • AVE ≥ .50
    • CR between .70 and .95
    • Cronbach’s α ≥ .70
    • Corrected item–total r ≥ .30

    Loadings come from a one-factor principal axis factoring of each construct’s items. They are close to, but not the same as, CFA loadings; confirm with CFA or PLS-SEM before publishing.

    Kaiser (1974); Fornell & Larcker (1981); Hair, Black, Babin & Anderson (2019); Nunnally & Bernstein (1994).

    Survey data

    One row per respondent, one column per item, with a header row. Name items with a construct prefix and a number (PU1, PU2, VA1…) so they group automatically. ID columns and text columns are ignored; rows with missing values are dropped. Comma, semicolon or tab separated.

    Discriminant validity

    Are the constructs empirically distinct from one another, or are two scales measuring the same thing?

    Fornell–Larcker: √AVEᵢ > |rᵢⱼ| HTMTᵢⱼ = mean hetero-trait r ÷ √(mono rᵢ · mono rⱼ)

    Criteria

    • √AVE of each construct exceeds its correlation with every other construct
    • HTMT < .85 (strict), < .90 (liberal)
    • Each item correlates most with its own construct (cross-loadings)

    Uses the same survey dataset as the Construct test. Construct correlations are computed from mean item scores.

    Fornell & Larcker (1981); Henseler, Ringle & Sarstedt (2015).

    Internal validity

    Can the observed effect be attributed to the cause you studied, rather than to something else?

    Score = Σ ratings ÷ (2 × applicable threats) addressed = 2 · partly = 1 · not = 0 g = (M₁ − M₂) ÷ s_pooled · J

    Criteria

    • Score ≥ 75% and no applicable threat left unaddressed
    • Baseline difference |g| ≤ .05 equivalent; .05–.25 adjust statistically; > .25 not equivalent
    • Attrition: report overall and differential rates; high differential attrition biases group comparisons

    Shadish, Cook & Campbell (2002); What Works Clearinghouse Procedures and Standards Handbook, v5.0 (2022).

    External validity

    Will the findings hold for other people, settings, treatments and times beyond this study?

    χ² = Σ (O − E)² ÷ E, df = k − 1 w = √(χ² ÷ n) residual = (O − E) ÷ √E

    Criteria

    • Sample does not differ from the population: p ≥ .05 and Cohen’s w < .30
    • No category with |standardised residual| > 2
    • Expected count ≥ 5 in every category
    • Generalisability review score ≥ 75%

    Bracht & Glass (1968); Shadish, Cook & Campbell (2002); Cohen (1988).
